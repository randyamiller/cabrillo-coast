What we do

Advisory that moves the big decisions in the right direction.

Most costly software problems are decisions, not bugs — the wrong boundary, the premature rewrite, the platform you can't leave. We help you make those calls with clarity, and stand behind them.

Strategic technical direction without a full-time hire — roadmap, hiring, and the hard trade-offs, from someone who's shipped.

System & Software Architecture

Architecture & Code Review

AI & Agentic Systems

Agentic development — with judgment, not just hype.

The teams pulling ahead aren't the ones with the most AI. They're the ones who wired it in deliberately — with the guardrails and evals to actually trust it. That's the work we do.

Agentic development

Design and ship LLM-powered agents — in your product and in your codebase — with the guardrails, evals, and human-in-the-loop escalation that make them dependable, not just demos.

Agentic workflows

Reshape how your team builds: coding agents, automated review loops, and orchestration that compress cycle time without loosening the bar on correctness.

Domain-specific harnesses

Custom eval, test, and simulation harnesses tuned to your domain — so agents and engineers alike get caught by the edge cases that generic tools sail right past.

Signature practice

Human–agent team topology

Adding AI agents to a team isn't an org-chart footnote — it's a redesign. We map who does what, where judgment lives, and how work and authority flow between people and agents — so AI makes your team faster and safer, not just louder.

  • Deliberate rolesPeople where judgment and accountability matter; agents where leverage and tireless throughput pay off.
  • Explicit review gates & escalationEvery agent output has an owner and a path up — no unreviewed work reaching production, no ambiguous authority.
  • Runnable, not theoreticalThe topology ships with the harnesses, guardrails, and evals that keep it honest as it scales.

About

Named for the coast we navigate from.

Juan Rodríguez Cabrillo charted this coastline without a map. Building software can feel the same way — the terrain is unfamiliar, the stakes are real, and confident navigation matters more than raw speed.

Cabrillo Coast LLC is a boutique advisory practice for teams building serious software. We bring senior, hands-on experience to the decisions that shape a system for years — and we're small on purpose, so the person you talk to is the person doing the work.

Led by Randy Miller — 30 years in software and cloud architecture: global high-availability systems, SRE, evergreen operations, and domain-specific languages, with a current focus on the topological design of high-performing human–agent teams.
